---
title: Gothic 1 Crazy Trainer
description: A trainer and mod menu for Gothic 1 — everything from one in-game menu.
---

Stats, inventory, item and NPC spawner, teleports, ESP, radar, party management, arena, free aim, "fun" modes (spell rain, meteors, zombie apocalypse, polymorph…) and much more. It runs in the Steam version of Gothic 1 with **Union** and **GD3D11**.

<Callout type="warn">Single-player only, and still in testing — back up your saves before you start.</Callout>
