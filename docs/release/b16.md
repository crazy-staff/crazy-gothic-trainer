## Installation
1. Download **G1CrazyTrainer_build16.zip** (below, in *Assets*) and unpack it.
2. Copy all files into `Gothic\system\Autorun` (replace the old ones).
3. Start the game with **Union + GD3D11**, load a save, press **Insert**.

> The in-game menu is in Polish for now — English is planned.

## Build 16
- **New tab Character › Combat:** attack speed (with a weapon drawn), infinite ammo, **free aim** (melee / bows / spells), **Aimbot** (key, delay, priority, field of view, range, smoothing, target lock, targets behind walls) and **Trigger bot** (range, angle, reaction time, interval, attack keys) — a switch between them, targets chosen with checkboxes.
- **Mode switches in one card:** No-clip | Glide, Ghost mode | Weapon only, Aimbot | Trigger bot. Turning one on turns the other off.
- **Visuals › Widgets:** all widgets in one dock (screen corner, width, opacity): health/mana, radar, threats, weapon, FPS, information. **Compass** at the top of the screen with character markers.
- **ESP: arrows around the crosshair** and **markers on the screen edges**; **Who is it attacking** (text and arrow to the fight target).
- **Visuals › Crosshair:** editor (shape, gap, length, thickness, rotation, constant spin, colour, dot, outline, opacity), 4 ready presets + your own.
- **Visuals › Graphics:** brightness, gamma, contrast, saturation, temperature, sharpening, vignette, filters (black and white, sepia, night vision, negative).
- **Settings in tabs** + menu scale, opacity, rounding, dimming the game behind the menu.
- **Screenshots:** F11 full screen, **Shift+F11 selected area**; clipboard + `G1CrazyTrainer_zrzuty` folder only (no automatic attaching).
- **Party:** fight target check every 0.5 s, **Diagnostics › Party test** (test scene).
- **Apocalypse** (formerly Zombie) and **Polymorph**: options split into separate cards; names "… of the apocalypse", story undead excluded, mortality set again after insertion.
- The pickup bot also returns to its spot after being stopped; auto-pickup without animation in no-clip/glide.
