## Installation
1. Download **G1CrazyTrainer_build14.zip** (below, in *Assets*) and unpack it.
2. Copy all files into `Gothic\system\Autorun` (replace the old ones).
3. Start the game with **Union + GD3D11**, load a save, press **Insert**.

> The in-game menu is in Polish for now — English is planned.

## Build 14
- **Glide and no-clip fully separate** — each has its own card and settings (speed, turning, mouse, sensitivity, ignored by enemies).
- **Glide:** new movement in 20 cm steps — a wall steeper than the set angle stops you (sliding along it), an allowed steep wall is climbed instead of throwing you off; no more sinking into the ground. New option **Collide with objects** (trees, rocks, bridges, grates).
- **Party:** party members no longer fight each other (wolf + human), a party monster does not bite neutrals and neutrals do not attack it. "Stop fighting after teleport" works for monsters too. Fixed the crash when spawning a wolf.
- **New tab Characters › Surroundings:** everyone around (including party and dead), filters and the same actions as for the target: target, teleport, pull, party, calm, hostile, freeze, kill, revive, remove.
- **New tab Visuals › Radar:** characters, items, chests; rotation with the hero, size, range, position, names, details on hover.
- **Preview** at the top of every Visuals tab — you see right away how ESP / radar / colours will look.
- **Max everything** as a button in the Skill level card header.
- **Win+Shift+S:** after pressing the Windows key the game releases mouse and keyboard for 8 s (Tools › Diagnostics).
