## Installation
1. Download **G1CrazyTrainer_build17.zip** (below, in *Assets*) and unpack it.
2. Copy all files into `Gothic\system\Autorun` (replace the old ones).
3. Start the game with **Union + GD3D11**, load a save, press **Insert**.

> The in-game menu is in Polish for now — English is planned.

## Build 17
- **Free aim (bow, crossbow, spells):** over-the-shoulder camera, mouse up/down aims, the arrow / spell flies at the character under the crosshair (also up and down), target highlight. Melee removed.
- **Aimbot / trigger bot:** require free aim; the aimbot turns the hero with the game engine (no crash) and puts the crosshair on the target; new keys (side mouse buttons, middle, Caps Lock, V) instead of Alt/Shift.
- **Crosshair:** 4 shapes (cross, dot, circle, corners), each with only its own settings; dot with size and shape; colour in settings and in presets; corners without overlapping lines.
- **Widgets:** separate "what to show" for radar and compass, preview in screen proportions (nothing cut off), new: target health bar, ammo, experience; Information: frame time graph, trainer time, memory, character and item counts, heading, time of day.
- **Graphics:** GD3D11 settings (draw distances, world, fog, sun, shadows, wind, scattering) + **graphics presets** (ready and your own).
- **ESP:** "what to show" as checkboxes; edge marker where the box left the screen; weapons in hands and arrows/bolts hidden by default.
- **Screenshots:** F11 and Shift+F11 save the whole screen including the menu; the image is frozen while selecting, the menu does not move.
- **Party:** a monster no longer stays locked on its old target; test scene with more characters.
- **Fun › Combat:** all apocalypse and polymorph options in their own cards (sections inside); "Can be killed" off = immortal.
- **Tester hub:** history folded to 5 entries, the mouse wheel scrolls the form.
- Removed the bot's "Return to spot" option.
