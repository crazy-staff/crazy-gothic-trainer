## Installation
1. Download **G1CrazyTrainer_build15.zip** (below, in *Assets*) and unpack it.
2. Copy all files into `Gothic\system\Autorun` (replace the old ones).
3. Start the game with **Union + GD3D11**, load a save, press **Insert**.

> The in-game menu is in Polish for now — English is planned.

## Build 15
- **Glide:** object collision worked the other way round (wrong raytrace flag) — fixed; objects without collision (grass, decorations), items and characters are skipped. Enemies really ignore you in glide/no-clip (also those already fighting).
- **Party:** whoever joins during a fight with you stops fighting; a monster does not bite you after a teleport. New **Fights** card: whether the party fights each other, whether party monsters/humans attack neutrals, whether neutrals attack your monster. From the party list: portrait and remove from the world.
- **One-click portrait** in Target and Surroundings. Compendium: **delete all icons and remake them** / delete portraits.
- **Inventory › Pickup:** auto-pickup (range, how many at once, interval, min value, animation, categories with checkboxes), **list of all items on the map** with coordinates, teleport and CSV export, **pickup bot** (shortest route, Esc stops, returns to the spot).
- **Zombie apocalypse and polymorph:** many new options (kinds, health, protection, distance, polymorph targets, effects, remove all zombies).
- **Radar:** "what to show" as checkboxes; the preview rotates like the real radar.
- **Search boxes remember the text** after switching tabs (can be turned off in Settings).
- **Game screenshot (F11):** saves a PNG, copies to the clipboard and attaches to the report automatically — instead of Win+Shift+S, which the game blocks.
