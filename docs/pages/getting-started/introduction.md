**Gothic 1 Crazy Trainer** is an in-game trainer and mod menu for the Steam version of Gothic 1. Press **Insert** in the game and you get one menu with everything: your hero's stats and look, cheats, no-clip, free aim, an item and NPC spawner, a party of companions, teleports, ESP and radar, an arena, a zombie apocalypse and a lot more.

> **Warning:** Single-player only. The trainer is still in testing — back up your saves before you start.

## What is inside

| Section | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Character** | stats, skills, look, cheats, no-clip and glide, free aim, aimbot |
| **Inventory** | spawn any item, ready-made kits, automatic pickup and an item map |
| **Characters** | actions on the NPC you look at, a party of companions, everyone around you, NPC spawner |
| **Fun** | arena with waves, zombie apocalypse, polymorph, meteors, spell rain, chaos |
| **World** | time of day, chapters, world speed, teleports |
| **Visuals** | ESP for characters and items, radar, compass, widgets, crosshair, graphics |
| **Tools** | console, compendium of portraits and icons, diagnostics |
