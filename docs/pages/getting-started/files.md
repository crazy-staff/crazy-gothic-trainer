---
title: Files the trainer creates
description: What is stored next to the DLL in system\Autorun.
---

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `G1CrazyTrainer.ini` | settings and hotkeys |
| `G1CrazyTrainer.log` | log — attach it when reporting a crash |
| `G1CrazyTrainer_zgloszenia.txt` | history of your reports and ratings |
| `G1CrazyTrainer_raporty\` | exported reports for the author |
| `G1CrazyTrainer_zrzuty\` | screenshots (F11) |
| `G1CrazyTrainer_obrazki\` | character portraits and item icons — safe to delete, they regenerate |
| `G1CrazyTrainer_celowniki.txt`, `G1CrazyTrainer_grafika.txt` | your crosshair and graphics presets |
