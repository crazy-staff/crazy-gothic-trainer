The trainer draws **portraits of characters** (when you aim at them or save them as a target) and **icons of items** (for the spawner and lists). The compendium shows them all.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Browse** | every portrait and icon, searchable |
| **Make icons of all items** | renders icons for every item (about a minute; Esc stops) — also in Settings |
| **Delete all icons and remake** / **delete portraits** | start fresh if pictures look wrong |

Pictures are stored in `G1CrazyTrainer_obrazki` — safe to delete, they come back.
