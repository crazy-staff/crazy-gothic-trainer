Ways to move around the world: no-clip (fly through walls), glide (walk on the ground without physics), hero speed and sprint. No-clip and glide cannot be on at the same time.

> **Note:** In the menu: **Postać › Ruch** (the in-game menu is in Polish for now).

## No-clip (flying)

Through walls; WASD, mouse turns, Space/Ctrl up/down, Shift x4; Esc turns it off.

**Type:** On / off · **ID:** `noclip`

## Speed

M/s (Shift x4).

**Type:** Value · **ID:** `ncspeed` · **Default:** 9 (range 1 – 100)

## Q/E turning

Degrees per second.

**Type:** Value · **ID:** `ncturn` · **Default:** 130 (range 20 – 720)

## Mouse turns the hero

Moving the mouse sideways turns the hero, up/down tilts the camera.

**Type:** On / off · **ID:** `ncmouse`

## Mouse sensitivity

**Type:** Value · **ID:** `ncmsens` · **Default:** 1 (range 0.2 – 4)

## Enemies ignore you

Monsters and enemies do not notice you in no-clip.

**Type:** On / off · **ID:** `ncignore`

## Glide (on the ground)

Walks on the ground without physics and animations; WASD, Shift x4; Esc turns it off.

**Type:** On / off · **ID:** `glide`

## Collide with objects

Trees, rocks, bridges, grates, fences — you walk onto them instead of passing through.

**Type:** On / off · **ID:** `glcoll`

## Max slope

Degrees; steep walls up to this angle are climbed (90 = climbs any wall vertically).

**Type:** Value · **ID:** `glideslope` · **Default:** 50 (range 0 – 90)

## Speed

M/s (Shift x4).

**Type:** Value · **ID:** `glspeed` · **Default:** 6 (range 1 – 60)

## Q/E turning

Degrees per second.

**Type:** Value · **ID:** `glturn` · **Default:** 130 (range 20 – 720)

## Mouse turns the hero

**Type:** On / off · **ID:** `glmouse`

## Mouse sensitivity

**Type:** Value · **ID:** `glmsens` · **Default:** 1 (range 0.2 – 4)

## Enemies ignore you

**Type:** On / off · **ID:** `glignore`

## Hero speed

Animation speed = running, attacks, turning.

**Type:** Slider · **ID:** `pspeed` · **Default:** 1.5 (range 0.2 – 5)

## Sprint

Running overlay from the speed potion.

**Type:** On / off · **ID:** `sprint`
